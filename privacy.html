Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

This policy covers parallelleaf.com, the website of Parallel Leaf LLC ("Parallel Leaf", "we", "us"). Our apps have their own privacy policies, linked from each app's page.

The short version

This website doesn't track you. It has no analytics, no advertising, no cookies and no sign-in. We don't sell or share information about visitors.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub records your IP address for security purposes, as it does for every GitHub Pages site. We don't have access to those records. GitHub handles them under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Emails you send us

If you write to contact@parallelleaf.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you. We retain emails for up to two years, after which they are deleted, unless we must keep them longer for legal or accounting reasons.

Your rights

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. This includes requests under laws such as the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Children

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
